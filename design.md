# House of Avira - Design & Aesthetic Guidelines

## 1. Core Aesthetic Direction
- **Vibe:** Creative, Gen Z, Pinterest-Coded, Edgy, Authentic.
- **Anti-Vibe:** Traditional Stiff Luxury, Corporate Minimalism.
- **Visual Elements:** Scrapbook-style collage layouts, bold overlapping typography, intentional asymmetry, high-contrast imagery, sticker-like overlays, and "handwritten" annotations.

## 2. Color Palette
The color scheme is stark, high-contrast, and edgy.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** The primary canvas. It keeps the interface clean and allows user-generated style content and bold typography to pop.
- **Pure Black (`#000000`):** Used for heavy, brutalist typography, thick borders, and high-contrast UI components.
- **Deep Cherry Red (`#8A001A`):** The signature accent color. Used for hover states, selection highlights, active icons (like the wishlist Heart), and primary call-to-actions. It adds a grunge-adjacent, trendy Gen Z flair.

## 3. Typography
We are moving away from traditional elegant serifs (like Playfair) to more expressive, impactful fonts.
- **Bold Big Normal (Primary Headline Font):** 
  - *Examples:* Syne, Space Grotesk, Archivo Black, or Anton. 
  - *Usage:* Massive, unapologetic, tightly-spaced headlines. It should look loud and confident.
- **Cursive / Handwriting (Accent Font):** 
  - *Examples:* Caveat, Kalam, Permanent Marker, or Nothing You Could Do. 
  - *Usage:* Used for annotations, subtitles, and "hand-written" notes scattered across the UI. This provides the essential "Pinterest mood board" feel.

## 4. Educational UX (Customer Transparency)
A core part of the Gen Z aesthetic is radical authenticity. We must boldly educate the customer about the business model rather than hiding it in the fine print.
- **Preorder Transparency:** Prominently display *"This is a preorder business"* via scrolling marquees, top banners, and product page callouts.
- **Import & Shipping Realities:** Clearly communicate that products are imported and shipping is charged separately. Use creative UI components (like a "handwritten" sticky note or a bold badge on the product page) to explain this.
- **Policy Honesty:** State *"No cancellations or refunds after ordering"* confidently and upfront. Designing these policies into the actual aesthetic (e.g., a "READ ME" sticker) makes the brand feel trustworthy and transparent.
